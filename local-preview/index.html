What changed structurally

The old content shape was feature-heavy but shallow. It had one overview page and two large feature pages, with detailed dashboard notes mixed directly into the main reading path.

The current shape separates:

  • Entry points for first-time readers
  • Task-oriented investigation workflows
  • Reference material for metric-by-metric interpretation
  • A troubleshooting home for future FAQ and support paths

This is the right direction if the goal is a fuller product documentation experience rather than a short dashboard note set.

<\!-- How to validate -->

How to validate this structure

  • Check whether the navigation groups match how your users think.
  • Confirm each page has a clear job and does not duplicate another page.
  • Look for gaps where the structure exists but the real product content still needs to be added.
  • Use this page for IA review, then open individual pages in VS Code Markdown Preview for wording review.

Suggested validation checklist

  • Does Overview feel like the right first page for a customer or operator?
  • Should APM and infrastructure be sibling feature hubs, or should there be a broader “Monitor and investigate” landing page first?
  • Do you want troubleshooting to remain a lightweight FAQ, or should it become a fuller runbook section?
  • Are there missing top-level product areas such as alerts, synthetic monitoring, dashboards, or operational best practices?
  • Do the current page boundaries match how Adobe support, CSEs, and customers actually navigate incidents?
